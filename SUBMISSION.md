Title: Pet Home Match

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable bilateral pet-home workflow. A shelter freezes public care needs for one pet, one designated applicant declares a home, routine, and constraints, and GenLayer validators return a need mask plus MATCH, REVIEW, or NO_MATCH. The shelter then shortlists or declines, and the applicant separately confirms or withdraws before a placement record. No protected or private traits are inferred and no placement is automated.
